The checkout page has a promo field, and that field is the only place a code proves itself. ## The cost that never appears on a coupon page

Lightlayer's stated rule for price changes is that existing orders and ongoing services are excluded, while new orders get the changed pricing. Sign up, and a later price revision will not move the invoice on your running server. That protects you from increases, and it also means the number you agree to is the number you keep paying while that service runs.

Renewals are a separate question, and it is the one coupon directories ignore. Some Lightlayer promotions have explicitly carried the same price into renewal; the November 2024 cloud flash sale was one, advertised at $2/month with the same renewal price. Others say nothing about renewal. On a discounted plan, look for a renewal sentence in the offer text before you commit to a long term.

The other lever is billing cycle length. In June 2024 Lightlayer ran a renewal discount that scaled with commitment: 10% off for one month, 20% for three, 30% for six and 50% for twelve, with the renewal period changeable in the client area. That campaign is over and those percentages should not be expected to return, but the mechanism is still in the account panel. Changing your renewal cycle before the invoice generates is the one renewal action available to every customer, on every plan.
